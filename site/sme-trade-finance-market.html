Loss rate a year (%)Credit losses a yearNet a yearReturn

Income is trade financed times the margin per cycle. Credit losses are the loss rate times capital deployed (capital times utilisation). Net is before funding, FX and operating costs. Return is net divided by capital allocated. The margin applies per cycle, so shorter wholesale cycles usually carry a lower margin per cycle; set it to match the segment. Every facility is subject to assessment.

How to read this

Short import cycles mean the same capital funds several shipments a year. A 90 day cycle at 85% utilisation turns capital about 3.4 times.

Wholesalers turn stock faster: they buy, sell to shops on short credit and repay from weekly sales, so a 45 day cycle turns capital about 6.9 times. Much of their stock is bought locally in TSh, so less of it goes through an FX desk.

Questions traders ask


What is a typical SME import shipment in Tanzania?
SME importers commonly bring in shipments of around USD 15,000 to 20,000, often a mix of consumer goods or building materials from China. Sizes vary widely by sector.


When is demand for import finance highest in Tanzania?
Demand usually builds ahead of year end and the January school opening, and October is often the busiest month as traders stock up for Christmas.


What currency do Tanzanian importers pay suppliers in?
About 80% of supplier payments are made in US dollars, with yuan, dirhams and other currencies making up the rest.


How long is a typical SME import cycle?
From supplier deposit to goods in the shop often takes two to three months for sea freight from China. Short cycles let the same capital fund several shipments a year.
