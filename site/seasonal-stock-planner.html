      Questions traders ask
      

      When are the busiest selling seasons in Tanzania?
      The main peaks are Christmas and year end, school opening in January, Ramadan and Eid, Easter, the upcountry harvest season from around June and the mid year school term. Ramadan and Eid move earlier each year with the lunar calendar.
      

      What sells more at school opening?
      Exercise books, pens, uniforms, school shoes and bags usually move fastest in the weeks before schools open in January, and again before the mid year term.
      

      What sells more during Ramadan and Eid?
      Dates, sugar, rice, cooking oil, flour and kitchenware usually sell more during Ramadan. Clothing, shoes, perfume and gifts pick up before Eid.
      

      How early should I order for Christmas?
      Work back from early December by your full lead time. Local supply may need about three weeks, while imported goods often need two to three months, so many wholesalers order imports by September.
