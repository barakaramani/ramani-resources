Business trip to Burundi from Tanzania

Tanzanian passport holders do not usually need a visa for Burundi for visits of up to 3 months, and direct flights from Dar es Salaam to Bujumbura take about 2 to 3 hours. In 2024 Tanzania imported USD 997,000 of goods from Burundi, making it the 93rd largest supplier, led by vehicles and parts; coffee, tea and spices; and soap and detergents.

              Questions traders ask

              Do Tanzanians need a visa for Burundi?

              No. Tanzanian passport holders do not usually need a visa for Burundi for visits of up to 3 months. You still need a passport valid for your whole stay. This is as of 6 Nov 2026. Rules change, so confirm with the embassy or high commission of Burundi, or its official immigration website, before you book.

              How long is the flight from Dar es Salaam to Bujumbura?

              Direct flights from Dar es Salaam to Bujumbura take about 2 to 3 hours. Prices and schedules change, so compare fares on Google Flights or Skyscanner before you book.

              What do Tanzanian traders buy in Burundi?

              Burundi is mostly a market for Tanzanian goods. In 2024 Tanzania imported USD 997,000 of goods from Burundi, making it the 93rd largest supplier, and the biggest categories were vehicles and parts; coffee, tea and spices; and soap and detergents.

              When is the best time to visit Burundi for business?

              Business runs all year. Check trade fair and public holiday dates before you book flights.
