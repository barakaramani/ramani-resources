Community rules

Safe, useful and respectful

  • Kiswahili first. Respect every member.
  • No selling, no loan offers and no links from members. Only Ramani posts links.
  • Ramani will never ask you for money, PINs or passwords in the community. Report anyone who does.
  • Answers about financing are general and subject to assessment. Personal questions move to a private message.
  • Keep it about importing: suppliers, shipping, clearing, stock and growth.
Questions

Frequently asked

Is Biashara Tanzania free?

Yes. Membership, the courses, the market updates and the meetups are free.

Who can join?

Importers only: businesses that bring in their own goods, through full containers or shared loads, and people planning their first import.

Do I need an app?

No. Biashara Tanzania works in any phone or computer browser. You can add it to your home screen like an app.

Do I have to borrow from Ramani?

No. The community is for learning and connecting. If you want stock finance later, Ramani assesses your business separately.

Who can see my details?

Members see the name and business on your profile. Your phone number stays private. Ramani never sells your details.
