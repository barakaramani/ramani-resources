      How it works
      

      1. Register as an agent with your licence number.
      2. Refer a client who has agreed to it and say whether they need duty, supplier payment or both.
      3. Ramani calls the client within one working day and you earn commission on funded deals.
      

      Questions traders ask
      

      Who can join the referral programme?
      Licensed clearing agents and freight forwarders in Tanzania. Tools open straight away, and referral payouts start once your licence has been checked.
      

      Will the finance provider take over my client's clearing?
      No. Ramani never takes over the clearing of a client you referred, and every facility is subject to assessment.
      

      How is referral commission paid?
      Commission is earned on deals that are funded, at a rate agreed in advance, and paid to your mobile money or bank account.
      

      Do I need my client's permission to refer them?
      Yes. Only refer clients who have agreed to be contacted, and let them know who will call.
