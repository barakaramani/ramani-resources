              What is holding my container?

              Common causes at Dar port and what usually fixes each one.

              Missing or wrong documents

              Ask your agent which paper is missing. Get the supplier to correct the invoice or packing list and send a scan the same day.

              Duty and VAT not paid

              Pay the assessed amount through your bank or mobile money with the control number from TRA. Release cannot start until it shows as paid.

              TBS inspection or test results

              Book the inspection early and send the certificate of conformity if you have one. Without it, goods may wait for testing and you may pay extra fees.

              Shipping line has not released it

              Pay the local shipping line charges and hand over the original bill of lading, or confirm a telex release. Then collect the delivery order.

              Moved to an ICD

              Busy weeks can move your container to an inland container depot. Ask your agent which ICD, then pay its charges as well. ICD storage runs on its own clock.

              Customs physical check

              If your declaration goes to the red channel, customs opens the container. Make sure your agent attends and that the goods match the packing list.

              Value or description query

              TRA may question a low value or an unclear description. Send your proforma, contract and proof of payment so the query closes fast.

              Permit from another authority

              Food, cosmetics, medicines and farm inputs may need TBS, TMDA or TPHPA permits. Apply before the ship arrives, not after.

                    Questions traders ask
                    

                    What is a reorder point?
                    It is the stock level at which you should place your next order. It equals the stock you sell during the lead time plus a safety buffer, so new goods arrive before you run out.
                    

                    How do I calculate a reorder point?
                    Multiply units sold per week by the weeks from order to shelf, then add safety stock. Selling 50 units a week with an 8 week lead time and 4 weeks of safety stock gives a reorder point of 600 units.
                    

                    How much safety stock should I keep?
                    It depends on how steady your sales are and how reliable shipping is. Goods from far away usually need a bigger buffer than local purchases, and many traders hold two to four weeks for fast sellers.
                    

                    What lead time should I use for imports from China?
                    Count every step: production, waiting for a vessel, sea freight, clearing and transport to your shop. By sea from China this is often around two to three months, so check against your own past orders.
